Our Services

Consultation-led cannabis care

Everything below is informational. Cannabis products are dispensed exclusively to patients with a valid PT 33 prescription issued during an in-clinic consultation — nothing here can be ordered or purchased online.

🩺

New Patient Consultation

A licensed practitioner reviews your medical history and current symptoms to determine whether cannabis therapy is appropriate for you.

🔄

Follow-Up & Renewal

PT 33 prescriptions are valid for a single fill, up to 30 days. Returning patients can book a follow-up to review response and renew.

📚

Patient Education

One-on-one guidance on dosing, method of use, and what to expect — especially helpful for first-time patients.
