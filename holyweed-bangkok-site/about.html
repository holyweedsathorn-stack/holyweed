Our Story

Cannabis medicine, taken seriously.

HOLYWEED began as one of Bangkok's early cannabis spaces. As Thailand's regulatory framework matured into a medical-only model, we did too — rebuilding around licensed physicians, proper documentation, and patient safety, while keeping the warmth that made people feel at home in the first place.

🎯

Our Mission

Make medical cannabis access in Bangkok safe, well-documented, and genuinely helpful — never a hard sell, always a health decision made with a licensed practitioner.

⚖️

Our Compliance Standard

We operate as a licensed medical cannabis clinic under Thailand's Ministry of Public Health framework: PT 33 prescriptions only, GACP-certified sourcing, and full prescription record-keeping.

🤝

Our Patients

From chronic pain to sleep and anxiety-related conditions, we work with each patient's doctor-led care plan — not a one-size-fits-all product list.
