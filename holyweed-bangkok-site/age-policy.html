Age Policy

Last updated August 2026

HOLYWEED Bangkok enforces a strict minimum age of 20 years, consistent with Thai law governing access to cannabis products. This policy applies to both this website and our physical clinic locations.

Accessing this website

By browsing this site, you're confirming that you're 20 years of age or older. We don't run a third-party identity check to verify your age online — this is an honesty-based confirmation, similar to age notices used across the web. Misrepresenting your age to access this site is a violation of our terms of use.

Visiting our clinics

Verification is stricter in person. A valid government-issued ID or passport is required at the door for every guest at both our Sathorn and Sukhumvit 22 locations, without exception. Our staff are trained to check identification and will decline consultation or service to anyone unable to provide valid ID, or who appears to be under 20.

Reporting a concern

If you believe this policy has been violated, please let us know at holyweed.sathorn@gmail.com.

The bottom line: 20 and above, no exceptions. Please consume responsibly and only as directed by a licensed practitioner.
