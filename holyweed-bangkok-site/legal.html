Legal Notice & Terms of Use

Last updated August 2026

This page explains what this website is, what it is not, and the terms that apply to anyone using it. Please read it alongside our Age Policy and Privacy Policy.

1. Nature of this website

This website provides general, informational content about HOLYWEED Bangkok and medical cannabis care in Thailand. In line with Thai cannabis marketing regulations, this site does not sell cannabis products online, does not display pricing, and does not run promotional offers for cannabis. Nothing on this site constitutes an offer to sell cannabis, and no order or transaction for cannabis products can be completed here.

2. Not medical advice

Content on this site — including blog articles, strain descriptions, and formulary listings — is educational only and does not constitute medical advice, a diagnosis, or a prescription. It is not a substitute for an in-person consultation with a licensed practitioner. Cannabis products are dispensed only to patients presenting a valid PT 33 prescription issued by an authorized practitioner during an in-clinic consultation.

3. Age restriction

This website and both of our clinic locations are restricted to individuals aged 20 and above, consistent with Thai law. See our Age Policy for full detail on how this is applied online and in person.

4. Accuracy of information

We make a reasonable effort to keep information on this site current, including facts about Thailand's cannabis regulations. However, cannabis law in Thailand continues to evolve, and we cannot guarantee that every page reflects the most recent regulatory change at the moment you read it. This site is not a substitute for independent legal advice — if you need a legal opinion on your specific situation, please consult a licensed Thai attorney.

6. Limitation of liability

To the extent permitted by Thai law, HOLYWEED Bangkok is not liable for decisions made based solely on information found on this website without an in-person consultation.
